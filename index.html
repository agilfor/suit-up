suit-up

Use your camera to become Iron Man!

So basically I keep seeing people making Iron Man suits online, and it inspired this project as I'm not smart enough to build one myself.

Currently I'm only at the stage of face landmarking with mediapipe, but the ultimate goal is to overlay a 3D Iron Man model over whoever is on camera.

Use SPACE to toggle the cameo on/off.

If you like this, take a look at the other stuff I'm working on!
